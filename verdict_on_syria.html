#001 The 1962 Census & The Arab Belt (1962-1976)
5 October 1962
1962 Census: 120,000 Kurds Stripped of Citizenship Syrian Ba'athist State & President Nazim al-Qudsi

A one-day special census stripped 120,000 Syrian-born Kurds of their citizenship overnight.

Human Rights Watch documented that Decree No. 93 stripped 20% of the Syrian Kurdish population of their citizenship, turning them into 'foreigners' (ajanib) and stateless people (maktumin) in the country of their birth.

RESULT: Created a permanent underclass of over 300,000 stateless Kurds denied basic human and civil rights.
