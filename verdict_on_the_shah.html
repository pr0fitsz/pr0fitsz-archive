#002 Reza Shah: Centralist State & Assimilation Doctrine
1928-1929
1928 Uniform Dress Law & Prohibition of Tribal Attire Reza Shah Pahlavi & 7th Majlis

Cabinet decree and Majlis statute criminalizing traditional Kurdish and tribal garments, mandating Western suits, and forcing the Pahlavi cap under penalty of cash fines and imprisonment

As documented by Encyclopaedia Iranica (Columbia University, Vol. V, Fasc. 8), on 26 September 1928 the cabinet decreed that all males dress uniformly in Western style, replacing traditional cloaks, felt coats, and tribal costumes with European suits, and forcing the Pahlavi cap under threat of cash fines and detention. On 27 December 1928, the parliament ratified the law making the uniform dress regulations legally compulsory across towns and rural tribal provinces.

Result: Took away Kurdish and nomadic tribes of their indigenous dress and cultural identity, subjecting anyone wearing native costume to police harassment, cash fines, and arrest.
